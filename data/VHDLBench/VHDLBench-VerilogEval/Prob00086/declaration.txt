Declaration:
    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support multi-valued logic types for representing digital signals and buses. The std_logic_1164 package provides the fundamental std_logic and std_logic_vector types used for modeling hardware signals with multiple logic states including high, low, high-impedance, and unknown values. Additionally, the numeric_std package is included to enable arithmetic operations and type conversions on std_logic_vector types, though the core LFSR functionality primarily relies on bitwise operations and vector slicing available through the standard logic types.
