Declaration:
    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support the fundamental digital logic types and operations used throughout the module. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is necessary to provide the std_logic type for representing single-bit signals with multiple logic states, including the standard binary values and metalogical states. This package also supplies the rising_edge function used to detect positive clock transitions in synchronous logic. No additional arithmetic, numeric conversion, or specialized packages are required for this design, as it operates exclusively on individual logic bits without performing mathematical operations or vector manipulations.
