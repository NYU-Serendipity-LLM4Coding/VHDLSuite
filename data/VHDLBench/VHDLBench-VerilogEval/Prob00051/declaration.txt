Declaration:
    The design requires the IEEE standard logic library to provide the fundamental logic types and operations used in digital design. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is needed to support the std_logic and std_logic_vector types, which represent single-bit and multi-bit digital signals respectively. This package also provides the standard logical operators (and, or, xor) that are essential for implementing the combinational logic gates in this design. No additional libraries or packages beyond the IEEE standard logic support are necessary for this purely combinational circuit.
