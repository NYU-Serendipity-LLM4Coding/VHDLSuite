Declaration:
    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support the fundamental digital logic types and operations used throughout the module. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is necessary to provide the std_logic type for representing individual bit signals and the std_logic_vector type for representing multi-bit buses. These types enable the representation of the five single-bit input signals and the twenty-five-bit output vector. Additionally, the package supplies the logical operators needed for the design, including bitwise inversion (not), exclusive-or (xor), and concatenation (&) operations that are applied to construct intermediate signal vectors and compute the final comparison results.
