Implement a module that reverses the byte order of a 32-bit vector.

Module name:
    TopModule

Input ports:
    signal_in: 32-bit input vector to be byte-reversed (bits 31 downto 0)

Output ports:
    signal_out: 32-bit output vector with reversed byte order (bits 31 downto 0)

Implementation:
    This module performs a byte-order reversal (endianness conversion) on a 32-bit input vector. The operation is purely combinational with no clock or reset signals required.

    The 32-bit input signal_in is treated as four consecutive 8-bit bytes. The module rearranges these bytes such that:
    - The least significant byte of signal_in (bits 7 downto 0) becomes the most significant byte of signal_out (bits 31 downto 24)
    - The second byte of signal_in (bits 15 downto 8) becomes the second byte of signal_out (bits 23 downto 16)
    - The third byte of signal_in (bits 23 downto 16) becomes the third byte of signal_out (bits 15 downto 8)
    - The most significant byte of signal_in (bits 31 downto 24) becomes the least significant byte of signal_out (bits 7 downto 0)

    The transformation occurs instantaneously through concurrent signal assignment, making this module suitable for data format conversion between big-endian and little-endian representations in communication protocols or memory interfaces.
