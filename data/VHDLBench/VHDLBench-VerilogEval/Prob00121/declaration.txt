Declaration:
    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support multi-valued logic types for ports and internal signals. The std_logic_1164 package provides the fundamental std_logic and std_logic_vector types used for single-bit and vector signals throughout the module. Additionally, the numeric_std package is referenced to enable potential arithmetic or type conversion operations on logic vectors, though the primary functionality relies on standard logic operations and comparisons for state encoding and transition logic.
