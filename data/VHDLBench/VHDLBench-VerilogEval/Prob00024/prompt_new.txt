Implement a module that performs half addition. A half adder adds two single-bit binary inputs and produces a sum output and a carry-out output.

Module name:  
    TopModule

Input ports:
    a: First single-bit operand (std_logic, 1-bit)
    b: Second single-bit operand (std_logic, 1-bit)

Output ports:
    sum: Sum result of the addition (std_logic, 1-bit)
    cout: Carry-out from the addition (std_logic, 1-bit)

Implementation:
    The module implements a combinational half adder circuit. It performs binary addition of the two input bits a and b without any carry-in. The addition operation produces a 2-bit result where the least significant bit represents the sum and the most significant bit represents the carry-out. The sum output reflects the XOR of the two inputs, while the cout output reflects the AND of the two inputs. This is a fundamental building block in digital arithmetic circuits.
