Declaration:
    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support the fundamental digital logic types and operations used throughout the module. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is necessary to provide the std_logic type for representing individual signal states, including the standard values for logic levels and high-impedance conditions. This package enables the declaration of single-bit ports and internal signals with appropriate resolution functions for multi-driver scenarios, and supports the logical operations and comparisons performed within the state machine logic.
