Declaration:
    This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support multi-valued logic types including std_logic and std_logic_vector, which are essential for representing digital signals and buses. The numeric_std package is also required to enable arithmetic operations on std_logic_vector types through unsigned and signed conversions, supporting operations such as addition, subtraction, comparison, and type casting to and from integer values. These standard libraries provide the foundational types and operations necessary for implementing the branch predictor's pattern history table indexing, saturating counter arithmetic, and history register manipulation.
