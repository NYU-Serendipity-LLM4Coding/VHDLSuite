    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to provide the fundamental logic types and operations for digital design in VHDL. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is needed to define multi-valued logic signals including std_logic and std_logic_vector types, which represent the digital signals and buses in the circuit. Additionally, the numeric_std package is required to support arithmetic operations on unsigned integer representations, enabling the implementation of counters and numerical comparisons within the design. These standard IEEE libraries provide the necessary type definitions and operations for modeling synchronous sequential logic, state machines, and arithmetic functions in hardware description.
