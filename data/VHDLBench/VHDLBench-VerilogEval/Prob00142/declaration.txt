    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support multi-valued logic types representing digital signals and their operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is needed to define the std_logic type for modeling individual signal states and the std_logic_vector type for representing buses or groups of signals. These standard types enable the representation of binary values, high-impedance states, and other signal conditions commonly used in digital hardware design. No additional arithmetic or numeric conversion packages are required for this particular design, as it relies solely on enumerated state types and basic logic operations.
