    This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support fundamental digital logic types and operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package provides the std_logic and std_logic_vector types necessary for representing individual bits and bit vectors used throughout the module's interface and internal signals. Additionally, the numeric_std package is essential for arithmetic operations, as it supplies the signed and unsigned types along with their associated arithmetic operators, comparison functions, and conversion utilities. These packages enable the design to perform signed and unsigned arithmetic computations, bitwise logical operations, shift operations, and numerical comparisons required by the ALU functionality. No non-standard or project-specific libraries are assumed or required for this design.
