Declaration:
    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support the fundamental logic types used in digital design, including the std_logic type for representing individual bits and std_logic_vector for representing multi-bit buses. Additionally, the design uses the IEEE numeric_std package to enable arithmetic operations on unsigned vectors, including increment operations and numeric comparisons with integer literals. These packages provide the necessary type definitions and operator overloading to perform unsigned arithmetic on vector signals and to convert between unsigned and std_logic_vector representations for port assignments.
