    This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support fundamental digital logic types and operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package provides the std_logic and std_logic_vector types used for representing single-bit and multi-bit signals throughout the design. Additionally, the numeric_std package is essential for performing arithmetic operations on vector types, enabling unsigned arithmetic, vector resizing, and conversions between std_logic_vector and unsigned representations. These libraries form the foundation for implementing synchronous digital logic with parametric bit-widths and arithmetic functionality.
