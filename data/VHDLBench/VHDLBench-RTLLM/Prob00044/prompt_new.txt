Implement a clock generator entity that produces a periodic clock signal, toggling its output state at regular intervals defined by a constant period value.

Entity name:
    clkgenerator

Input ports:
    None

Output ports:
    clk: The output clock signal generated by the entity, of type std_logic.

Implementation:
    The entity generates a continuous clock signal using an internal signal that is initialized to logic low. A clock generation process creates the periodic waveform by toggling the internal signal at half-period intervals. The constant PERIOD is set to 10 nanoseconds, resulting in a complete clock cycle of 10 ns (5 ns high, 5 ns low). The internal signal starts at logic '0' and continuously toggles in an infinite loop, with each state transition occurring after waiting for half the period duration. The internal clock signal is then assigned to the output port to provide the generated clock to external components. This architecture produces a square wave clock signal with a 50% duty cycle at the frequency determined by the PERIOD constant.
