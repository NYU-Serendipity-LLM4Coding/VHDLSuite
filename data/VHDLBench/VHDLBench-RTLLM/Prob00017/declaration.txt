    This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to provide fundamental digital logic types and operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package supplies the std_logic and std_logic_vector types used for representing signals and ports in the design. Additionally, the numeric_std package is necessary to enable arithmetic operations on unsigned vector types, including addition and subtraction of magnitude values, as well as comparison operations between unsigned operands. These standard IEEE libraries provide the essential infrastructure for implementing fixed-point arithmetic with proper handling of sign-magnitude representations and vector-based numeric computations.
