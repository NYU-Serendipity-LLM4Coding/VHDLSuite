    This design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support fundamental digital logic types including std_logic and std_logic_vector, which are used for representing single-bit and multi-bit digital signals respectively. Additionally, the IEEE numeric_std package is required to enable arithmetic operations on unsigned vector types, specifically supporting increment and decrement operations on the internal waveform counter. These standard VHDL libraries provide the necessary type definitions and operators for implementing synchronous digital circuits with reset capabilities and arithmetic functionality.
