This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support digital logic types and operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package provides the fundamental logic types including std_logic and std_logic_vector, which are used for representing individual signals and multi-bit buses in the design. Additionally, the numeric_std package is required to enable arithmetic operations on unsigned vector types, including addition and left-shift operations that are essential for computing partial products and accumulating intermediate multiplication results in the pipelined datapath.
