    This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support multi-valued logic types and vector operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package is necessary to provide the std_logic type for single-bit signals and the std_logic_vector type for multi-bit buses. These types enable the representation of digital signals with defined logic levels and support for vector slicing operations used throughout the hierarchical adder structure. No additional arithmetic packages or non-standard libraries are required, as the design implements arithmetic functionality through explicit Boolean logic expressions rather than relying on arithmetic operators.
