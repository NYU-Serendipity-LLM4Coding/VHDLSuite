This VHDL design requires the IEEE standard logic library to support digital logic types and operations. Specifically, the std_logic_1164 package provides the fundamental logic types including std_logic and std_logic_vector, which are used for representing individual signals and multi-bit buses respectively. Additionally, the numeric_std package is required to enable arithmetic operations on vector types, including support for unsigned integer representation, multiplication, addition, and resizing operations that truncate or extend bit-widths as needed for accumulation computations.
